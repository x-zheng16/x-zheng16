## Xiang Zheng

[Homepage](https://x-zheng16.github.io) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=lqirTjkAAAAJ) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-2990-2169) · [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/profile?id=~Xiang_Zheng4) · [Email](mailto:xiang.zheng@cityu.edu.hk)

I am a Research Assistant Professor at the Hong Kong Institute of AI for Science (HKAI-Sci), City University of Hong Kong, and work closely with [Prof. Xingjun Ma](https://xingjunm.github.io) at the [Institute of Trustworthy Embodied AI](https://teai.fudan.edu.cn), Fudan University.
My research develops robust and efficient reinforcement-learning algorithms for trustworthy science, computer-use, and embodied agents.

I was honored to receive the [CityUHK Presidential PhD Scholarship](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/media/news/2020/06/16/prestigious-new-scholarships-hk156m-world-class-phd-candidates).

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from City University of Hong Kong in 2024, advised by [Prof. Cong Wang](https://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/~congwang/); my M.S. in Control Science and Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2019, advised by [Prof. Tao Zhang](https://www.au.tsinghua.edu.cn/info/1081/3196.htm); and dual B.S. degrees in Automation and Mathematics from the Shen Yuan Honors College, Beihang University, in 2016.
