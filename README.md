## Xiang Zheng

[Homepage](https://x-zheng16.github.io) / [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=lqirTjkAAAAJ)

I am a Research Assistant Professor at the Hong Kong Institute of AI for Science (HKAI-Sci), City University of Hong Kong, and work closely with [Prof. Xingjun Ma](https://xingjunm.github.io) at the [Institute of Trustworthy Embodied AI](https://teai.fudan.edu.cn), Fudan University.
My research develops robust and efficient reinforcement learning and recursive self-improving algorithms for trustworthy science, computer-use, and embodied agents.
